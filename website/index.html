Remind. Do. Log. Repeat.

A logbook that only records is a spreadsheet with better fonts. A calendar that ignores what you did is just an alarm. ReptiKeep is one mechanism, and what you log moves what it asks for next.

It tells you what is due.

One notification, at a time you pick, listing the day's chores across the whole collection. Feed Basil, change water for six of them, clean out Ziggy. If you would rather be told animal by animal, that works too.

You log it afterwards.

Nobody writes it down with their hands still in the enclosure. ReptiKeep stores when something happened separately from when you typed it in, so backdating is the ordinary path rather than a workaround you have to go looking for.

It counts from the last log.

You pick the interval once, weekly, every ten days, whatever suits the animal. The next reminder counts forward from the last record you logged rather than from a fixed calendar date, so feeding a day late moves the next one instead of building a backlog.

Made for iPhone and Android.
