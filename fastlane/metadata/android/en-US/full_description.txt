ReptiKeep is a husbandry log for reptile keepers. Feed. Shed. Repeat.

Log feedings, weigh-ins, sheds, waste, water changes and enclosure cleaning for every animal in your collection, and let the app tell you who needs attention today.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Give each reptile its own profile: species, sex, photo, birth date and the day you got them
• Log six kinds of activity: feeding, shed, waste, weight, habitat upkeep and medical care
• Backdate records, because nobody logs things in real time while holding a snake
• See a per-animal timeline with feeding status and a weight trend chart
• Set feeding, water-change and enclosure-cleaning schedules that fit your routine
• Check one Reminders screen for everything overdue or due today across your whole collection
• Get an optional daily notification instead of hunting for what's due
• Attach documents like vet records or certificates of authenticity to an animal
• Back up your whole collection to a file and restore it on the same or a new device
• Use it in English or Brazilian Portuguese, in light or dark mode

BUILT AROUND YOUR PRIVACY
ReptiKeep works fully offline. No account, no sign-up, no data leaving your device unless you choose to export it yourself. It's also open source, so you, or anyone, can read exactly how it works.

WHO IT'S FOR
Built for keepers with a real collection, whether that's three geckos or thirty snakes across a rack. Once memory alone can't keep track of who ate when, ReptiKeep can.
